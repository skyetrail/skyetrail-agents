## The three fixes to make first

1. **F4 — forbid weakening a check to make a fix pass.** The skill's whole job is applying other
   people's suggestions to code, and nothing in it stops an agent from loosening an assertion when a
   suggested change breaks a test. Highest consequence of anything here, and one sentence closes it.
2. **F3 — close the two lists that carry the skill's weight.** "Forbidden Responses" names three
   phrases and stops, so "Nice catch!" passes the check the skill exists to impose. "Push back when"
   names six cases and stops. Write the membership test first, then the examples, or end each list
   by generalising to its category as line 143 already does.
3. **F2 — state what the skill does not cover.** The prohibitions are absolute and unbounded, so
   they read as governing every exchange rather than code review. Adding the boundary also resolves
   the dependent `skill § Boundary` row, and pairs naturally with F6 and F8, which add the person's
   override and the successor skill at the same edge.

F1, stating the finished outcome and what settles it, is the next one after these three.
