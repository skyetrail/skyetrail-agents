# Skyetrail plain-English rules

The rules below apply to prose the agent writes. They do not apply inside code spans, code blocks, file paths, identifiers, frontmatter fields, or text quoted from another source.

- No em dashes. Reword with a period, a comma, or a connector such as "such as", "since", or "including".
- No en dashes, including in number ranges. Write a range with "to", e.g., "0.94 to 0.96".
- Use simple, everyday words. Prefer the common word, e.g., "use" rather than "leverage". Avoid words that AI tools overuse, e.g., "delve", "robust", and "landscape".
- Repeat a word rather than reaching for a synonym just to avoid repeating it.
- Write complete sentences with a subject and a verb. Do not write fragments.
- Use a colon only to introduce a list. Do not use a colon to join clauses or to set up a point.
- Use straight quotes, not curly quotes.
- Avoid jargon. If a technical term is truly needed, say it once and explain it in plain words.
- Do not use analogies, metaphors, or imagery. Describe the actual thing. Avoid the "not just X, it is Y" pattern.
- Cut filler, and cut an "-ing" tail that adds fake analysis.
- Do not coin hyphenated adjectives to sound compact. Write them out in plain words.
- Do not make an inanimate thing take an action verb. Make a person the actor where one exists.
- Do not pad with empty emphasis or puffery, e.g., "really", "matters", or "pivotal".
- Use sentence case in headings. Do not bold the first phrase of every bullet.
- Be optimistic.
