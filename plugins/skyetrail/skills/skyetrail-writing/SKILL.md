# Skyetrail writing style

> **Superseded.** As of 2026-08-21, this project checks writing style with the ai-tells Vale ruleset, not this skill. See `../../../steering/shared/ste.md` for the rules now in force. This file stays because other things still cite it.

Write everything in clear, everyday language. The goal is text that is easy to read, not like an AI tool generated it. Plain does not mean short. Clarity comes first.

## Checklist before you finish
- No em dashes. Reword so the sentence flows without one, using a period, a comma, or a connector such as "such as", "since", or "including".
- No en dashes, including in number ranges. Write a range with "to", e.g., "0.94 to 0.96".
- Use simple, everyday words. Prefer the common word, e.g., "use" rather than "leverage". Avoid words that AI tools overuse, e.g., `delve`, `robust`, and `landscape`.
- Repeat a word rather than reaching for a synonym just to avoid repeating it.
- Write complete sentences with a subject and a verb. Do not write fragments.
- Use a colon only to introduce a list. Do not use a colon to join clauses or to set up a point.
- Use straight quotes, not curly quotes.
- Avoid jargon. If a technical term is truly needed, say it once and explain it in plain words.
- Do not use analogies, metaphors, or imagery. Describe the actual thing. Avoid the "not just X, it is Y" pattern.
- Cut filler, and cut an "-ing" tail that adds fake analysis, e.g., "stores results, highlighting its value".
- Do not coin hyphenated adjectives to sound compact. Write them out in plain words.
- Do not make an inanimate thing take an action verb. Make a person the actor, e.g., "you can search the logs".
- Do not pad with empty emphasis or puffery, e.g., `really`, `matters`, or `pivotal`.
- Use sentence case in headings. Do not bold the first phrase of every bullet.
- Be optimistic.

## How to apply
Draft the content, then read it against the checklist before you finalize it. Pay closest attention to em dashes and jargon, since those are the most common slips.
