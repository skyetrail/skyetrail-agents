# Skyetrail writing style

Write everything in plain English. The goal is text that is easy to read, not like an AI tool generated it. Plain does not mean short. Clarity comes first.

## Core rules
Read the shared rules in [plain-english.md](../../shared/plain-english.md) and apply every one of them. This is a required step. Those rules are the core of the Skyetrail style, and they apply to every document, email, and post.

## How to apply
Draft the content, then read it against the rules in [plain-english.md](../../shared/plain-english.md) before you finalize it. Pay closest attention to em dashes and jargon, since those are the most common slips.
